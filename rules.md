# 1 Overview
1. A warning is a written or verbal message from a staff member directed at one or more members that clearly outline an action that do not comply with the rules.
2. The rules apply globally. This includes ingame, Discord, and private chats such as DMs or groupchats.
3. If you receive a punishment, it will also apply globally. For example, if you are muted ingame, you will also be muted on Discord. And vice versa.
4. The rules apply regardless of your state of being. Being drunk or high is not a valid excuse for commiting infractions.
# 2 Private Chats and Other Servers
1. Direct messages and group chats are considered private. This means you can do anything you want, given that you have the other parties' consent.
2. Private messages that are non-consensual can be considered harassment, and repeated offenses should be reported.
3. Voice channels within our Discord server are not considered private. Any infractions commited in our voice channels will be punished.
4. Our rules do not apply to other Discord servers. If you are being harassed in another Discord server, you are encouraged to leave the Discord server.
